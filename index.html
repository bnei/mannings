Uniform steady flow, Q = (1/n)·A·R^(2/3)·√S₀ with SI units (k = 1.0). Normal depth is found by bisection on a strictly increasing Q(y), so the root is unique.

Results assume the channel is prismatic and the flow uniform. Near-critical flow, backwater effects and non-uniform sections are outside what this calculates. briannei.com
